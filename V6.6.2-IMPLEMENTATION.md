# Scout v6.6.2 — Optimized Early Signal Architecture

v6.6.2 separates the first actionable heads-up from later confirmation.

Production tracing showed fresh_impulse and first_leg_release remained dominant blockers and that 5 of 10 recent promotions were late-risk. The new EARLY_SIGNAL path uses an evidence score while keeping hard safety gates intact.

EARLY_SIGNAL cannot bypass actionable CLEAN/bullish quality, participation, structure, bearish-short rejection, structural-failure rejection, extension ceiling, trigger proximity, or candidate freshness. It may notify before the legacy fresh_impulse / first_leg_release pair completes when the evidence score is strong enough.

Later SURGE, BREAKOUT, IGNITION, RECLAIM, and REARM logic remains unchanged and acts as confirmation.

Each promoted finding records the early_signal score, evidence, hard blockers, velocity, acceleration, extension, trigger distance, and whether the path was used.
