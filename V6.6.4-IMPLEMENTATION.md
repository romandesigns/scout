# Scout v6.6.4 — Canonical Late-Risk Guard

This release closes the EYPT-style EARLY_SIGNAL bypass.

EARLY_SIGNAL now treats Scout's shared `is_late_promotion_risk()` result as a hard
blocker. A candidate above the canonical 0.75% base-extension boundary (or 2.0%
extension boundary) cannot become a new EARLY actionable promotion through the fast
path, even when it remains under EARLY_SIGNAL's separate 0.90% extension ceiling.

The v6.6.2 early architecture, v6.6.3 fresh BREAKOUT/IGNITION/SURGE late suppression,
and explicit re-entry paths remain unchanged.
