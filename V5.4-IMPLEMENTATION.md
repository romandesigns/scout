# V5.4 implementation notes

## Practical changes

- Default scanner range is `$0.15-$10.00`.
- Settings can change and persist the range at runtime. The server refreshes the universe and reconciles live SIP/BOATS subscriptions.
- Findings, gainers, notifications, and desktop/mobile lists enforce the active range.
- The inspector includes candidate-profile radar, quality radial, velocity bar, and participation composed charts.
- Validation includes average follow-through and 30-minute time-of-day signal/follow-through charts.

## Extracted from the supplied five-step stock-selection lesson

Scout now exposes prior close and gap, current session volume, projected full-session volume, and current shares per minute. These feed the candidate profile alongside catalyst, structure, tape participation, velocity, and the existing anti-chop quality gate.

The lesson also emphasizes daily 50-day RVOL and float under 10 million shares. Scout does not fabricate either value. Intraday RVOL remains based on live baseline buckets, and float is displayed as unavailable until a trusted reference-data provider is configured.

These metrics are decision-support signals, not promises of performance or automated trade advice.
