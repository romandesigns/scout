# Scout v6.6.6 — Continuation-Quality Selectivity

v6.6.6 is the first forward-outcome-informed selectivity release.

Two independent v6.6.5 quality cohorts showed that Scout's timing was fast but many
EARLY fast-path promotions did not sustain follow-through. The new gate is deliberately
narrow: it only affects `EARLY_SIGNAL`, leaving confirmed BREAKOUT/IGNITION, explicit
re-entry/reclaim behavior, canonical late-risk protection, and the quality-audit
pipeline unchanged.

An EARLY_SIGNAL now needs at least one continuation-quality route:
- first-leg context, or
- relative-activity context, or
- strong velocity plus positive reacceleration, or
- pristine quality (100) with meaningful velocity and positive acceleration.

The captured production examples that motivated the rule are covered directly:
- SNWV-style strong reacceleration is preserved.
- context-backed early signals are preserved.
- RSKD/EYPT-style weak, uncontextual/decelerating fast-path releases are blocked.

This does not guarantee profitability. It is intended to reduce weak fast-path alerts
while preserving the early timing architecture already validated in production.
