# Scout v6.6.7 — A-Rank EARLY Precision + Version-Isolated Outcome Audit

v6.6.7 makes two evidence-driven changes.

1. Fresh EARLY notifications are A-rank only by default. B-rank EARLY candidates remain
tracked internally and may still promote later through confirmed BREAKOUT/IGNITION or
explicit continuation/re-entry paths. In the deduplicated mature captured cohort, A-rank
EARLY signals had materially better 5-minute return and MAE, while all observed EARLY
false positives were B-rank.

2. Detection-quality audit v4 filters by the currently deployed engine_version by default.
This prevents post-deploy reports from mixing older-version findings into the new release
cohort, which was contaminating immediate v6.6.6 evaluation.

No late-risk threshold, BREAKOUT/IGNITION rule, re-entry rule, or notification transport
behavior is loosened.
