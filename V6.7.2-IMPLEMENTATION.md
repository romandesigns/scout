# Scout v6.7.2 — Recall / Opportunity / Profitability Instrumentation

This release deliberately does **not** loosen or tighten the trading detector.

It adds the missing measurement layer exposed by WFF and IPST:

1. Right-tail recall for +5%, +10%, +20%, and +50% movers.
2. First market observation vs first Scout observation vs first actionable observation.
3. Percentage of the observed move already consumed when Scout becomes actionable.
4. Prospective session sampling of top gainers and the 24H panel.
5. A naive top-gainer baseline on the same sampled tape.
6. Rolling multi-report detection-quality aggregation across versions/sessions.
7. Explicit lists of the largest missed movers and largest late-actionable movers.

The intent is to prevent future "profitability" tuning from improving average
precision by accidentally removing asymmetric winners.

This is an observational optimization release. Detector gates, rank rules,
notification rules, 24H classification, and v6.6.9 continuation protection are
unchanged.
